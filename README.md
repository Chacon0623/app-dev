# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is my favorite series because it combines mystery, science fiction, horror, and adventure. I enjoy watching it because of its interesting story and unexpected events.

### About the Series

- **Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Drama
- **Created by:** The Duffer Brothers
- **Release Year:** 2016
- **Platform:** Netflix

### Why I Like It

1. The story is exciting and mysterious.
2. The characters have strong friendships.
3. Every episode makes me curious about what happens next.

### My Favorite Character

*Dustin Henderson* is my favorite character because he is funny, intelligent, and loyal to his friends.

### My Favorite Quote

> Legit to gagi!

### My Rating

**9/10** – I highly recommend this series to people who enjoy mystery and adventure.
